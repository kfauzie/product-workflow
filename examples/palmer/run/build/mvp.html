Which tickets can the AI resolve on its own?

Decision rules

  • Resolve alone: accuracy 98% or more, and the cost of a wrong call is Low or Medium.
  • Draft for approval: the agent drafts, a person approves. Accuracy 90% or more, or a High-cost type with accuracy 98% or more.
  • Human only: everything else, including any type that moves money with accuracy under 98%.
