# Customer personas: Palmer

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** Palmer, the use case and all data are synthetic. Any resemblance to a real company is unintended.

| Persona | Decisions they own | Wants | Pain points | Says yes to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head of Customer Support | Which ticket types the AI agent handles | Hours back without payroll mistakes | Can't show which types are safe; one wrong refund becomes the story | Evidence on their own tickets, a clear rule |
| Small-business owner (the end customer) | Runs payroll for 3 to 50 staff | Quick, right answers in the app | Being told the wrong thing about pay or tax | Answers that are right, or a person |
