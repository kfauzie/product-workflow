---

- **Need:** What's behind wanting auto-resolve on for more ticket types?
  - *<If vague → "Who's asking: your team, or leadership?">*
- **User journey:** Walk me through the last time you switched a category to auto-resolve: who decided, on what evidence, and what happened next?
  - I noticed Billing disputes is 87.5% accurate with 4.9% serious errors. What happens today when an agent gets one wrong?
  - *<Hedge words → "You said usually. Is there an exception?">*
- **Pain points:** Where does most of your team's time go? · What's stopped you switching more on so far?
- **Success criteria:** If this worked, how would you know? · What would your VP and Finance need to see to approve it?
- **Closing:** **Recap** three points, biggest problem first · Anything I missed? · Can I email you short questions?
