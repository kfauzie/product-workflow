# 3. Discovery notes (from the call)

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** Relaywise, Relay Agent, Relay Studio and Pellmore are invented. The use case and all data are synthetic. In a real run these pages live in Notion; here they are Markdown so the workflow can be read end to end.

- Leadership has set a target of "20% fewer agent hours next year"; she was asked to "turn on more automation".
- She switched Order status on last quarter on gut feel. It went fine, but she can't show why it was safe, so she won't do the same with bigger categories.
- Her fear is one bad AI answer on a refund going to a big customer: "that's the story people remember, not the hours saved".
- Billing disputes takes most of her senior agents' time; she'd love to automate it, but doesn't trust it yet.
- API errors spiked after a release; engineering is fixing the root cause, so she doesn't want to invest there.
- Success: Finance signs off a saving, and she can switch a category off quickly if quality drops.
- Trust: seeing accuracy per category, on their own tickets, and what a mistake would cost.
