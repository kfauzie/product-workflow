# Product glossary: Corvane

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** Corvane, Riskline, Retrofit Planner, Ashgrove Partners, Project Lantern and Lantern Group are invented. All data is synthetic. Any resemblance to a real company is unintended.

| Term | Plain meaning | Unit | Where it appears | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AAL (average annual loss) | Building damage to expect in an average year, weighting every possible event by its chance | £ a year | Data, screens | A forecast for one year |
| Overall grade | A–G ranking of damage as a share of rebuild cost | A (lowest) to G | Data | £ loss: a big building can be graded A and still lose a lot |
| Rebuild cost | What it would cost to rebuild the site | £ | Data | Market value, or the deal price |
| Scenario | A climate future, from low emissions (SSP1-2.6) to very high (SSP5-8.5) | — | Data, screens | A business case |
| Defended / undefended | Whether flood defences are assumed to protect the site | — | Data, screens | Insurance |
| EV (enterprise value) | The price of the whole company, including its debt | £ | Deal inputs | Equity (the investor's own money) |
| Hold period | How long the investor owns the company before selling it | Years | Deal inputs | The climate scenario's horizon |
| IC (investment committee) | The group that approves the deal | — | Customer's process | — |
| RAG status | Red / Amber / Green status each diligence workstream reports to IC | — | Screens | The overall grade |
