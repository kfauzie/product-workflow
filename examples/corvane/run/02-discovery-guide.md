# 2. Discovery guide (pm-2)

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** Corvane, Riskline, Retrofit Planner, Ashgrove Partners, Project Lantern and Lantern Group are invented. All data is synthetic. In a real run these pages live in Notion; here they are Markdown so the workflow can be read end to end.

**For prep:** the four findings in [01-data-review.md](01-data-review.md). AAL = damage to expect in an average year, in £.

---

- **Need:** What made you ask whether the output could be more useful to the deal side?
  - *<If vague → "Is it more about what it says, or when it arrives?">*
- **User journey:** Walk me through the last deal you sent an assessment on: when did it land, and what did the deal team do with it?
  - I noticed one site, the Port Avelin office, is 73% of Lantern's expected loss. What's behind that?
  - *<Unexpected step → "How did you end up handling that?">*
- **Pain points:** Where does most of the time go? · Has a climate finding ever changed a price or terms? *<If never → "What stopped it?">*
- **Success criteria:** If this worked on Lantern, what would the deal team do differently? · What would they need to see to trust the number?
- **Closing:** **Recap** three points, biggest problem first · Anything I missed? · Can I email you short questions?
