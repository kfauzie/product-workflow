**What we need to test:** the defence suggestions (right defences, right provider, "to verify" when the standard isn't known) and the drafted deal terms. About 15 key sites from 5 or 6 past deals.

1. Could your team have used this brief on Project Lantern? What would the deal team have done with it?
2. Will the deal team accept a defended figure if the defences are named, with who provides them and to what standard? What evidence would they want?
3. Could you send us the top 2 or 3 sites from 5 or so past deals, with what you know about their flood defences and any terms where climate came up? Please include one where the defences were disputed and one where the answer was "just insure it".
4. Are the premium assumptions credible, or should they come from a broker?
5. Would a spreadsheet of yearly cost lines drop into your model as is?
6. *(Optional)* By default the brief shows the defended case, the middle scenario, and the hold and EV from the deal record. Would your team use the same defaults?
