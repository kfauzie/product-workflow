# 1. Data review (pm-1, Initial review)

> **FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** Corvane, Riskline, Retrofit Planner, Ashgrove Partners, Project Lantern and Lantern Group are invented. All data is synthetic. In a real run these pages live in Notion; here they are Markdown so the workflow can be read end to end.

**The data:** 14 rows, one per site; per site, 9 hazards × 4 scenarios × 2 years (2030, 2050) of AAL in £, damage as % of rebuild cost, and a grade. Usable: losses recompute from % × rebuild cost, no missing cells. Flood is modelled **undefended**. Unless stated: 2030, middle scenario (SSP2-4.5).

| # | Finding | Why it might matter (guess) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One site carries three-quarters of the risk.** The Port Avelin office loses £7.1m a year of the £9.8m total (73%), 94% of it coastal flood. The top 3 sites carry 94% | The deal's climate question is one site, not 14 |
| 2 | **The overall grade hides the £.** That office is graded **A** (damage 0.79% of its £900m rebuild cost); 12 of 14 sites are graded A | Reading grades, a deal team would see "all fine" |
| 3 | **Over the hold it's today's risk.** Total AAL rises 4.1% from 2025 to 2035 | The case is about price now, not 2100 |
| 4 | **No link to the deal's money.** Rebuild cost is £1.56bn, 4.3× the £360m EV; no site value, earnings or ownership | £ loss can't yet be set against EV or the business plan |

**Questions**
